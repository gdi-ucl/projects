# Fit-for-purpose, affordable body-powered prostheses

Research into affordable upper-limb prostheses whose design responds to users’ activities and the environments in which they live.

![Upper limb body powered prosthesis consisting of a hand and an arm that has two sections joined by a mechanical elbow.
